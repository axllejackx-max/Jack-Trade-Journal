Why JACK Journal

A journal isn't a highlight reel. It's a record.

Most people open a journal after a good trade. JACK Journal is built for the record that comes before and after that — the setup you took, the reasoning behind it, the state of mind you were in, and what actually happened.

Not a signal. Not a shortcut. A better way to understand your own trading — one entry at a time, stored on your device, reviewed on your terms.

Core Principle
"Disciplined trading isn't built from one perfect trade. It's built from consistently reviewing the process."

Performance

See your trading, objectively.

The Equity Curve shows how your cumulative P/L develops over time, measured in R rather than currency.

It gives you a clearer, more objective view of your performance journey — reflecting the quality of your decisions, not the size of your account.

Every review — weekly, monthly, or by category — pulls directly from your logged trades. No manual tallying, no spreadsheets. Just an honest, up-to-date picture of what your process actually produces.

Real Trading Journey

JACK Journal helps you document every result objectively, so each trade — whatever the outcome — becomes material for review and learning.
